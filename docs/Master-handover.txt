What is KAAVALU?

KAAVALU is an intelligent radio-frequency monitoring and classification system.

It is designed to:

1. Receive radio-frequency IQ samples from an SDR, recorded IQ files or simulated signal sources.
2. Detect individual radio signals.
3. Analyze their physical characteristics.
4. Extract mathematical signal features.
5. Classify recognizable modulation types using machine learning.
6. Compare detected signals against configured authorization rules.
7. Identify unusual or potentially unauthorized activity.
8. Generate explainable alerts.
9. Display results through a monitoring dashboard.
10. Trigger physical alerts through an ESP32.

In simple language:

Imagine hundreds of invisible radio signals travelling through the air.

Different organizations use radio frequencies for communication.

KAAVALU examines the physical properties of these signals and helps identify what is present, what matches configured rules, and what deserves further human investigation.

It does not listen to conversations or decode communication content.

It is a spectrum monitoring and classification tool, not a jammer, transmitter or communication interception system.

SECTION 3 – OUR INNOVATION

Do not claim that radio signal detection, FFT, SDR or modulation classification are inventions of our team. These technologies already exist.

Our proposed differentiation is the integration of multiple capabilities into one offline, explainable spectrum-monitoring workflow.

Five innovation pillars

1. Intelligent signal identification

Instead of simply displaying radio-frequency activity, KAAVALU attempts to detect signals, extract their characteristics and classify their modulation.

2. Classification plus authorization checking

Our system asks two separate questions:

- What kind of signal are we observing?
- Does this signal match our configured authorization rules?

Signal recognition and authorization are not the same thing.

3. Human-first explainable alerts

The system should explain why a signal was flagged.

It should not blindly declare unfamiliar signals illegal.

4. DisasterNet emergency coordination

We adapt spectrum monitoring to an emergency-response scenario with five configured emergency communication corridors.

5. Offline operation and physical alerts

The system should operate locally without cloud AI dependencies and provide optional ESP32-based physical alert indicators.

Our strongest safety innovation

UNKNOWN DOES NOT MEAN ILLEGAL.

If a signal is unfamiliar, low-confidence or has SNR below 8 dB, it must be classified as UNKNOWN and sent for human review.

Never automatically classify such a signal as POTENTIALLY_UNAUTHORIZED.

Our strongest innovation story should emphasize:

INTELLIGENCE + TRUST + PRACTICALITY.

However, always distinguish proposed innovation from features actually implemented and tested.

Never exaggerate novelty.

SECTION 4 – NON-NEGOTIABLE TECHNICAL GUARDRAILS

These rules must never be violated.

4.1 Offline architecture

100% local runtime execution.

- No cloud LLM API.
- No cloud AI dependency.
- No internet dependency for core signal processing.
- Local models and local storage only.

Online internet research during development is permitted.

4.2 Privacy

- Never decode communication content.
- Never extract private voice or message payloads.
- Analyze physical spectral and envelope characteristics only.

4.3 Fairness

UNKNOWN != ILLEGAL.

- SNR below 8.0 dB means UNKNOWN.
- Unfamiliar modulation means UNKNOWN.
- Insufficient classification confidence means UNKNOWN.
- Incomplete evidence means human review.
- Do not automatically accuse a transmitter of unauthorized activity.

4.4 Data provenance

Every capture and dashboard view must explicitly identify the data source:

- SIMULATED
- RECORDED_IQ
- LIVE_SDR

Never represent simulated data as real-world captured data.

4.5 ESP32 limitations

Hardware:

- Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3 Sense.
- ESP32 SuperMini.

The ESP32 has only two permitted responsibilities:

1. USB Serial physical alert indicators.
2. Isolated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi/BLE RSSI environment scanning.

It cannot:

- Capture VHF/UHF IQ samples.
- Replace an SDR receiver.
- Perform the primary radio-frequency spectrum analysis.

Use USB Serial at 115200 baud.

4.6 Reliability

- No unhandled hardware disconnection exceptions.
- Software-only fallback must work.
- Missing teammate modules must not prevent a basic demonstration.
- Validate incoming and outgoing dictionaries.
- Avoid silently swallowing important programming errors.
